Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about using the Student Center.

All currently enrolled UH students taking classes at the Sugar Land campus can use the Student Center. Bring your Cougar Card, since you may be asked to show it at the front desk or when checking in for a reserved room.

The center is open Monday–Thursday 7:00 AM–10:00 PM, Friday 7:00 AM–6:00 PM and Saturday 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. It is closed on Sundays and university holidays. The live “Open now / Closed” status at the top of every page shows whether the center is open right now.

Use the Room Booking form. Choose one of the five rooms, a date up to 30 days ahead, a start time and how long you need it (up to 3 hours). The form checks availability and confirms your request.

Yes, covered drinks and light snacks are welcome in the lounge, common area and collaboration rooms. Please keep food out of the gaming area and quiet study zones, and throw away your trash before you leave.

Registered student organizations can request the Common Area or the Media & Presentation Room for meetings and events. Submit your request at least two weeks in advance through the front desk, or book the Media & Presentation Room using the booking form for smaller meetings.
