About the Center

The University of Houston is opening a new Student Center on the Sugar Land campus to serve as one central place for students to study, relax, meet classmates and find campus resources.

Inside you'll find quiet study areas, a lounge and game room, bookable collaboration rooms and a common area for events. Student services staff hold regular hours in the center, so help is never far away.

  • One place for facilities, services and events information
  • Spaces for solo study and group work
  • Weekly social, academic, career and wellness events
